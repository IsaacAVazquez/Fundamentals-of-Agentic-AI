## Limitations

- Reviews are self-selected public app store posts, not a customer population.
- The data holds no account, plan, spend or subscription-status information. Cancellation intent is stated in text, not observed, so this memo cannot size business impact in money terms.
- Labels come from a model checked against a 50-review golden set and a verifier sample, so some misclassification is possible. The category names overlap: playlist-loss quotes appear under several issues, including [ISS-other] and [ISS-playback].
- Of 660622 source rows [C1], 660144 were completed [C2] and 478 were quarantined [C3]; pending records number 0 [C4]. Quarantined records are excluded from every count. Complaint records total 286629 [C5].
- Partial months (2022-05 and 2023-11) may skew totals.
- The priority score equals the severity sum, so volume and severity are blended and the top three are close.
